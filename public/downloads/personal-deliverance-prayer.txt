PERSONAL DELIVERANCE PRAYER
Lumen Fidei — 30-Day Spiritual Formation Program
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

HOW TO USE THIS PRAYER

This prayer is designed to be prayed aloud. Work through each section slowly
and sincerely. Where you see [BRACKETED TEXT], speak the names, sins, wounds,
or experiences that come to mind from your own life. You may pause and write
them out before praying if that helps.

If you have completed the Deliverance Notebook (Workbook), your entries will
guide what you name in each section.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 1 — OPENING PRAYER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, I come before You in humility, faith,
and repentance. I believe that You are my Savior, my Redeemer, my Lord, and my
Deliverer. I believe that You died for my sins, that You rose from the dead, and
that Your Precious Blood is stronger than every sin, wound, curse, demon, false
agreement, unholy bond, and evil influence that has touched my life.

In Your holy name, and through the power of Your Precious Blood, I renounce
Satan, all his works, and all his empty promises. I reject every evil that I
have accepted through sin, ignorance, fear, rebellion, weakness, curiosity,
woundedness, or deliberate choice. I reject every evil that has entered my life
through my own sins, through sins committed against me, through the sins of my
family line, through occult practices, through unholy relationships, through
false vows, through unforgiveness, through trauma, through deception, or through
any other opening known or unknown to me.

Lord Jesus, I ask You to cover me with Your Precious Blood. Wash over my mind,
my body, my soul, my memory, my imagination, my heart, my home, my family, my
relationships, and everything entrusted to me. Let every place of darkness be
brought under Your authority. Let every place of bondage be brought under Your
Cross. Let every place of fear, shame, confusion, hatred, lust, pride,
rebellion, despair, or torment be touched by Your mercy.

Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God, spread your mantle over me and pray for me.
Saint Michael the Archangel, defend me in battle. Holy angels of God, surround
me, guard me, and drive far from me every evil spirit. All holy saints of God,
pray for me.

Lord Jesus, as I name these sins, wounds, bonds, and agreements, give me true
repentance, true renunciation, and true freedom. Amen.

PAGE 2 — PERSONAL SINS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of all my personal sins, especially
[NAME YOUR PERSONAL SINS].
I confess that these sins were wrong, and I do not excuse them, hide them,
defend them, or cling to them. I place them under Your Precious Blood and ask
You to forgive me, cleanse me, and give me the grace to make a good Confession
wherever Confession is needed.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce these sins. I reject every pleasure,
habit, false comfort, false identity, and false promise attached to them. I
break every agreement I have made with these sins, whether knowingly or
unknowingly, and I reject every spirit that has gained influence in my life
through them.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I ask Almighty God to bind every evil spirit connected to these sins, to break
its power over me, to cast it away from me, and to remove every claim,
attachment, influence, or foothold it has had in my life. Lord Jesus, close
every door I opened through these sins, and fill every place that has been
emptied with Your Holy Spirit.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 3 — UNFORGIVENESS, RESENTMENT, AND JUDGMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of all unforgiveness, bitterness, resentment,
anger, hatred, revenge, accusation, and sinful judgment. I acknowledge before
You the people I have refused to forgive, especially
[NAME THE PEOPLE YOU HAVE NOT FORGIVEN].

What happened to me mattered. The wounds were real. The injustice was real. But
I do not want unforgiveness to rule my heart, and I do not want bitterness to
give the evil one any place in me. Therefore, in obedience to You, and with the
help of Your grace, I choose to forgive those who hurt me. I release them into
Your hands. I release my demand for revenge. I release the debt they owe me,
and I ask You to judge rightly, heal deeply, and restore what can be restored.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce unforgiveness, bitterness, resentment,
hatred, revenge, accusation, and every sinful judgment I have made against
others. I break every unholy bond, attachment, agreement, and spiritual claim
formed through unforgiveness. I reject every spirit that has used these wounds
to keep me bound.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I ask Almighty God to bind every spirit of unforgiveness, bitterness, hatred,
revenge, anger, resentment, accusation, judgment, and hardness of heart, to
break its power over me, to cast it away from me, and to restore in me the
mercy, peace, and freedom of Christ.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 4 — FATHER, MOTHER, FAMILY, AND CHILDHOOD WOUNDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I bring before You the wounds connected to my father, my
mother, my grandparents, my family, my home, and my childhood. I especially
bring before You [WOUNDS CONNECTED TO YOUR FATHER], [WOUNDS CONNECTED TO YOUR
MOTHER], and [WOUNDS CONNECTED TO YOUR WIDER FAMILY].

I forgive those who failed to love me as they should have loved me. I forgive
those who wounded me, rejected me, neglected me, abandoned me, frightened me,
controlled me, used me, shamed me, or placed burdens on me that I was not meant
to carry. I release sinful judgment against them, and I place them into Your
hands.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every lie I believed because of these
wounds, especially [LIES YOU BELIEVED ABOUT YOURSELF FROM THESE WOUNDS]. I
reject the lie that I am unwanted, unloved, unsafe, abandoned, worthless, dirty,
rejected, forgotten, or beyond healing. I reject every false identity that
entered through these wounds.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I break every unholy bond, attachment, agreement, wound, and spiritual claim
connected to these family and childhood wounds. I ask Almighty God to bind every
spirit of rejection, abandonment, fear, shame, control, confusion, hatred,
despair, self-hatred, and orphanhood connected to these wounds, to cast them
away from me, and to restore in me the truth that I am a beloved child of the
Father.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 5 — SEXUAL SIN AND UNHOLY ATTACHMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every sexual sin I have committed in thought,
word, desire, imagination, memory, or action. I repent especially of
[YOUR SEXUAL SINS]. I bring before You every person connected to these sins,
especially [NAMES OF PEOPLE CONNECTED TO THESE SINS].

I repent of lust, impurity, pornography, fornication, adultery, fantasy,
seduction, masturbation, use of others, consent to being used, sexual
manipulation, sexualized attention, and every misuse of my body or the body of
another person. I repent of treating what is sacred as common, of seeking
comfort apart from love, and of using sexuality in a way that separated me from
You.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every unholy soul tie, bond, attachment,
agreement, claim, pact, vow, oath, covenant, memory, fantasy, and spiritual
influence formed through these sins. I renounce every unholy attachment to the
people I have named, to their past partners, to those who encouraged these sins,
and to every spirit connected to these sins.

If any unholy ritual, pact, oath, vow, curse, blood covenant, or occult bond was
formed through these sins, knowingly or unknowingly, I renounce it and break it
now in the holy name of Jesus Christ.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit of lust, impurity, perversion, shame, fantasy, seduction,
addiction, domination, abuse, confusion, and unchaste attachment, to break its
power over me, to cast it away from me, and to cleanse my body, mind, memory,
imagination, and heart. Lord Jesus, restore in me chastity, purity, reverence,
peace, and the freedom of a child of God.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 6 — SOUL TIES AND UNHEALTHY BONDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I bring before You every soul tie, unhealthy bond, and
disordered attachment that has given another person an ungodly hold over my
heart, my will, my emotions, my body, or my peace. I bring before You especially
[NAMES OR RELATIONSHIPS WITH UNGODLY BONDS].

I acknowledge that these bonds were not holy. I renounce every ungodly soul tie,
unhealthy attachment, emotional dependency, and disordered connection formed
through sexual sin, trauma, abuse, grief, occult involvement, idolatry of
persons, or any relationship where fear, control, or disordered love gave
another person ungodly influence over me. I release each person I have named
into Your hands. I release myself from every claim these bonds have exercised
over my mind, my emotions, my will, my body, and my spiritual life.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every soul tie, bond, tether, and unholy
agreement — spoken or unspoken — formed through these relationships. I reject
every spirit that gained access, influence, or torment over me through these
bonds.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to sever
every ungodly soul tie, to reclaim every part of my heart, soul, mind, will, and
body that was given away or taken through these bonds, and to restore what was
damaged. Lord Jesus, bind every spirit of soul bondage, unhealthy attachment,
emotional dependency, control, domination, grief bondage, and spiritual
manipulation, break its power over me, cast it away from me, and restore in me
love that is free, ordered, and wholly surrendered to You.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 7 — OCCULT, DIVINATION, MAGIC, AND FALSE SPIRITUAL POWER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every way I have sought power, knowledge,
healing, comfort, protection, control, or guidance apart from You. I repent of
and renounce [YOUR OCCULT OR NEW AGE PRACTICES].

I renounce Satan and every occult practice. I renounce divination, witchcraft,
sorcery, magic, tarot, astrology, psychic readings, mediumship, spiritism,
séances, Ouija boards, spells, charms, curses, energy healing opposed to Christ,
New Age practices, false prophecy, false light, false worship, and every attempt
to gain hidden knowledge or spiritual power apart from God.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break every pact, vow, oath, ritual, curse, spell,
seal, assignment, attachment, agreement, and spiritual claim connected to these
practices. I reject every spirit that entered, influenced me, deceived me,
tormented me, or claimed access to me through these sins.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I ask Almighty God to bind every spirit connected to occult involvement,
divination, witchcraft, sorcery, deception, fear, false healing, false prophecy,
false light, control, and spiritual confusion, to break its power over me, to
cast it away from me, and to close every door I opened through these practices.
Lord Jesus, fill every place touched by darkness with the light of the Holy
Spirit.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 8 — WORD CURSES, EVIL WORDS, AND FALSE NAMES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I reject every evil word, curse, insult, judgment, label,
false identity, and lie spoken over me by others or spoken by me against myself.
I especially renounce [LIES SPOKEN OVER YOU BY OTHERS] and [LIES YOU HAVE
SPOKEN OVER YOURSELF].

I reject the words that said I was worthless, unwanted, broken, dirty, stupid,
cursed, hopeless, rejected, impossible to love, beyond healing, or destined for
evil. I reject every false name that came through cruelty, abuse, rejection,
humiliation, fear, shame, or despair. These words do not define me. These lies
do not own me. These curses do not have authority over me.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break agreement with every word curse, false
judgment, evil label, and lie spoken over me. I renounce every spirit that used
these words to wound me, bind me, accuse me, shame me, confuse me, or keep me
from receiving the truth of God.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit of rejection, shame, condemnation, self-hatred, despair, fear,
confusion, worthlessness, and accusation connected to these words, to break its
power over me, and to cast it away from me. Lord Jesus, speak Your truth over
me, and let Your word be stronger than every lie.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 9 — VOWS, OATHS, PLEDGES, AND INTERIOR PROMISES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every sinful vow, oath, pledge, promise, or
interior agreement I have made apart from You. I renounce especially
[YOUR SINFUL VOWS AND INNER PROMISES].

I renounce every vow made from fear, hatred, pride, lust, despair, grief,
revenge, control, self-protection, or unbelief. I renounce vows such as "I will
never forgive," "I will never trust," "I will never need anyone," "I will always
be alone," "I will get revenge," "I belong to darkness," "I cannot be free," "I
am beyond help," and every similar vow spoken aloud or held silently in my
heart.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break every unholy vow, oath, pledge, promise,
agreement, curse, attachment, and spiritual claim connected to these words. I
reject every spirit that used these vows to bind my will, harden my heart,
distort my identity, or shape my future apart from God.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit connected to these vows, to break its power over me, to cast it
away from me, and to restore my will to the freedom and obedience of Christ.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 10 — IDOLATRY AND FALSE SOURCES OF SAFETY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every form of idolatry in my life. I repent of
making idols of [YOUR IDOLS — THINGS YOU TURNED TO FOR SAFETY, COMFORT, OR
IDENTITY APART FROM GOD].

I repent of looking for ultimate safety, identity, comfort, control, pleasure,
healing, power, approval, escape, or worth in anything other than You. I
renounce every false god, every false altar, every false refuge, every false
covering, every false comfort, and every false source of peace.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break every unholy attachment, agreement, claim,
and spiritual influence connected to idolatry. I reject every spirit that has
used these idols to draw me away from God, weaken my faith, enslave my desires,
or keep me from surrender.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit connected to idolatry, greed, control, lust, addiction, fear,
pride, false worship, and false comfort, to break its power over me, and to
cast it away from me. Jesus Christ, You alone are Lord, and I choose to worship
You alone.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 11 — PRIDE, REBELLION, AND CONTROL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of pride, rebellion, disobedience, stubbornness,
self-reliance, and control. I repent especially of [YOUR PATTERNS OF PRIDE,
REBELLION, OR CONTROL].

I repent of exalting myself, defending my sin, refusing correction, hiding from
the truth, blaming others, despising authority, resisting Your will, and trying
to rule my life apart from You. I repent of wanting my own way more than Your
will.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce pride. I renounce rebellion. I renounce
control. I renounce self-will. I renounce every refusal to obey God.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit of pride, rebellion, control, stubbornness, arrogance,
disobedience, self-exaltation, and hardness of heart, to break its power over
me, to cast it away from me, and to give me a humble, obedient, and teachable
heart.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 12 — GENERATIONAL SINS AND FAMILY PATTERNS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I bring before You the sins, wounds, disorders, and evil
patterns that have affected my family line. I repent of my own participation in
these sins and patterns, especially [GENERATIONAL PATTERNS YOU HAVE PARTICIPATED
IN].

I do not claim personal guilt for sins I did not commit, but I reject every evil
inheritance, every sinful pattern, every curse, every bondage, every wound, and
every demonic influence that has passed through my family line and affected my
life. I renounce family patterns of addiction, violence, abuse, occult
involvement, idolatry, adultery, abandonment, hatred, despair, suicide, greed,
deception, unbelief, mental torment, sexual sin, poverty of spirit, and every
pattern contrary to the kingdom of God.

In the holy name of Jesus, and through the power of His Precious Blood, I break
every curse, attachment, agreement, assignment, and spiritual claim connected to
the sins of my ancestors and my family line. I ask Almighty God to bind every
evil spirit connected to these generational sins and patterns, to break its
power over me and my family, and to cast it away from us according to His holy
will.

Lord Jesus, place Your Cross between me and every evil inheritance. Let Your
mercy be stronger than every sin of the past. Bless my family, living and dead,
according to Your mercy, and bring repentance, healing, deliverance, and
salvation wherever they are needed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 13 — ABUSE, TRAUMA, AND WOUNDED RESPONSES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I bring before You the places where I was abused, harmed,
violated, neglected, abandoned, humiliated, manipulated, controlled, or wounded.
I bring You especially [TRAUMATIC EVENTS OR EXPERIENCES OF HARM].

I do not call evil good. I do not excuse what happened. I do not take blame for
what was not my fault. I place every wound, every memory, every fear, every
humiliation, and every violation under Your Cross.

But I also repent of every sinful response that came from these wounds,
especially [YOUR SINFUL RESPONSES TO THESE WOUNDS]. I repent of hatred,
revenge, despair, self-hatred, destructive choices, isolation, bitterness,
unbelief, impurity, rage, mistrust, and every way I allowed pain to lead me away
from You.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every lie, vow, attachment, agreement, and
spiritual claim connected to these wounds. I reject every spirit that entered
through trauma, fear, rejection, shame, despair, confusion, hatred, rage,
self-hatred, or torment.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every evil spirit connected to these wounds, to break its power over me, to cast
it away from me, and to remove its influence from my mind, body, soul, memory,
imagination, and heart. Lord Jesus, enter these memories with Your light, heal
what has been wounded, and restore peace where there has been torment.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 14 — ADDICTIONS AND COMPULSIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every addiction, compulsion, dependency, and
destructive habit in my life, especially [YOUR ADDICTIONS AND COMPULSIONS].

I repent of turning to created things for comfort, escape, control, relief,
numbness, pleasure, or identity apart from You. I renounce every false comfort,
every false escape, every agreement with slavery, every agreement with secrecy,
every agreement with despair, and every agreement with shame.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break every unholy bond, attachment, agreement, and
spiritual claim connected to these addictions and compulsions. I reject every
spirit that used these habits to enslave my body, weaken my will, cloud my mind,
isolate me from others, or separate me from God.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit of addiction, compulsion, slavery, secrecy, shame, despair,
self-destruction, and false comfort, to break its power over me, to cast it away
from me, and to strengthen me in repentance, accountability, healing,
perseverance, and freedom.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 15 — FALSE RELIGION, SPIRITUAL MIXTURE, AND FALSE WORSHIP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of every participation in worship, rituals, prayers,
initiations, symbols, practices, or spiritual systems opposed to You. I renounce
especially [PRACTICES FROM FALSE RELIGIONS OR SPIRITUAL SYSTEMS].

I reject every false god, false spirit, false worship, false blessing, false
initiation, false covering, false protection, and false spiritual authority. I
reject every mixture of the Catholic faith with practices that do not belong to
Christ.

In the holy name of Jesus, I break every pact, vow, oath, ritual, attachment,
agreement, curse, and spiritual claim connected to false worship and false
religion. I reject every spirit that entered, influenced me, deceived me, or
claimed access to me through these practices.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit connected to false worship, idolatry, religious confusion,
deception, fear, and spiritual bondage, to break its power over me, and to cast
it away from me. I choose the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. I choose
Jesus Christ and the faith of His Church.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 16 — HARM I HAVE DONE TO OTHERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of the ways I have harmed other people. I repent
especially of [HARMS YOU HAVE CAUSED TO OTHERS].

I repent of selfishness, cruelty, manipulation, neglect, dishonesty, impurity,
betrayal, anger, gossip, rejection, abuse, contempt, and every failure to love.
I repent of the wounds I caused knowingly or unknowingly. I ask You to forgive
me and to show me where I need to confess, apologize, repair, restore, or make
restitution.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every sin and every evil influence
connected to the harm I have caused. I reject every spirit that encouraged me to
wound, use, deceive, control, reject, or despise others.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
every spirit connected to these sins, to break its power over me, to cast it
away from me, and to give me humility, courage, and charity.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 17 — DOMINANT SPIRITUAL PATTERNS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, I now renounce the dominant spiritual patterns that have
afflicted me, tempted me, tormented me, deceived me, accused me, oppressed me,
or influenced my life. I renounce especially [YOUR DOMINANT SPIRITUAL PATTERNS].

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce every spirit connected to these patterns.
I reject each one. I break agreement with each one. I break every claim,
attachment, assignment, influence, and foothold connected to each one.

Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind
each and every evil spirit connected to these patterns, to render them powerless
against me, to silence their accusations, to break their influence, to cast them
away from me, and to prevent them from returning.

I belong to Jesus Christ. I am marked by baptism. I am covered by the Precious
Blood of Jesus. I am not the property of the evil one.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PAGE 18 — FINAL PRAYER OF BINDING, DELIVERANCE, AND PROTECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lord Jesus Christ, in Your holy name and through the power of Your Precious
Blood, I ask Almighty God to bind every evil spirit that has come against me,
afflicted me, tempted me, tormented me, deceived me, accused me, oppressed me,
influenced me, or attached itself to me through any sin, wound, curse, trauma,
occult practice, false religion, family pattern, unholy relationship, sexual sin,
false vow, word curse, unforgiveness, idolatry, addiction, or agreement with
evil.

I ask You, Lord Jesus, to break every curse, pact, spell, hex, vex, seal,
assignment, trigger, trance, vow, demonic blessing, evil prayer, ritual bond,
false covering, unholy attachment, and every other demonic bondage sent against
me, formed by me, accepted by me, inherited by me, or placed upon me in any way.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, I renounce them all. I reject them all. I
break their power over me through the Precious Blood of Jesus. I ask Almighty
God to render every evil spirit powerless, to silence every accusation, to close
every door, to remove every claim, to cast every evil spirit away from me, and
to make them incapable of tempting, tormenting, afflicting, deceiving, or
harming me.

Lord Jesus, wash me in Your Precious Blood. Wash my mind, my body, my soul, my
heart, my memory, my imagination, my emotions, my desires, my dreams, my home,
my family, my relationships, my work, and all that belongs to me. Let this place
and every place under my care be made holy by Your presence and protected by
Your power.

Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God and Queen of Angels, cover me with your
mantle and pray for me. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend me in battle. Holy
angels and archangels, surround me, guard me, and drive far from me every evil
spirit. All holy saints of God, intercede for me before the throne of God.

Come, Holy Spirit. Fill every place that has been emptied. Fill me with faith,
hope, charity, purity, humility, obedience, courage, wisdom, peace, and love.
Restore in me the grace of my baptism. Strengthen me for Confession, prayer,
the sacraments, forgiveness, conversion, and a life fully surrendered to Jesus
Christ.

Lord Jesus Christ, I place myself under Your authority, under Your mercy, under
Your Cross, and under the protection of Your Precious Blood. Keep me faithful
to You, deliver me from evil, and lead me into the freedom of the children of
God.

Amen.
