Build a single interactive React scene called PotBandageScene.

The scene shows one large cracked pot centered on screen against a dark, painterly background. The pot begins with 3 visible cracks already present, and water is actively flowing from all 3 cracks from the very start.

The interaction is simple and state-based:

INITIAL STATE
- The pot already has 3 cracks
- Water flows continuously from all 3 cracks
- No bandages are applied yet
- There are 3 invisible clickable hotspots, one over each crack

CLICK STATES
When the user clicks the first crack:
- Apply a bandage over that crack
- The bandage text should read: Quick Fix

When the user clicks the second crack:
- Apply a bandage over that crack
- The bandage text should read: Quick Prayer

When the user clicks the third crack:
- Apply a bandage over that crack
- The bandage text should read: Quick Deliverance

IMPORTANT
- Each hotspot can only be clicked once
- Previously applied bandages must remain visible
- The scene must stay in the exact same composition the whole time
- No camera movement, no zoom, no screen transition

WATER
- Water should already be flowing from all 3 cracks before any click
- The water should continue flowing throughout the bandage states
- Use a simple looping animation that reads clearly as flowing water
- Add subtle splash/drip motion near the bottom

AFTER THE LAST BANDAGE
- Once all 3 bandages have been applied, wait about 700ms
- Then the pot should do a short stress or shake animation
- Then the pot should explode or burst apart dramatically
- Use a stylized burst, not realistic physics
- Show visible shards or broken pieces moving outward
- Add a brief water surge and optional dust/splash effect
- Keep the burst centered in the same exact scene
