Core Profile (Primary User)

This is not a typical Catholic formation user.

This is someone who is:

Spiritually distressed, confused, or searching

Often coming in through pain, not curiosity

Has likely experienced one or more of the following:

Occult involvement (tarot, reiki, psychics, manifestation, etc.)

Trauma (emotional, relational, sometimes severe)

Persistent sin patterns they feel stuck in

Anxiety, fear, or sense of spiritual oppression

A “something is not right” feeling they can’t explain

Spiritual State

They are usually one of these:

1. Returning Catholic (MOST COMMON)

Baptized but not practicing

Has been away for years

Carries guilt, confusion, or skepticism

Open… but cautious

2. Spiritually Curious / Wounded

May not fully trust the Church yet

Has explored:

New Age

self-help spirituality

therapy without spiritual grounding

Feels like:
“I’ve tried things… but nothing actually fixed this”

3. Practicing but Struggling

Goes to Mass

Prays somewhat

But feels:

stuck

spiritually attacked

not free

Age Range

Primary:
25–45

Secondary:
18–60

Why:

This is where most:

trauma processing

spiritual searching

identity questions

relationship wounds
happen

What brought them here?

Not:
“I want to grow spiritually”

More like:

“Something is wrong with me”

“I feel stuck”

“I tried everything else”

“I’m tired”

“I don’t feel close to God”

“I think something spiritual might be going on”

👉 This is HUGE:
They are not starting from clarity
They are starting from disorientation

2. What is the program connected to?
Officially:

Archdiocese of Chicago

Healing & Deliverance Ministry

Under clergy authority (Deacon Tom, priests, etc.)

But experientially (IMPORTANT):

It should NOT feel like:

a diocesan form

a parish class

a bureaucratic system

It should feel like:

👉 A guided path into healing and relationship with God

Best framing:

This is:

A ministry-backed formation journey

With real people supporting you (mentors)

Rooted in the Catholic Church

But experienced as personal and safe

Tone balance:

You are walking a line between:

Institutional trust (this is legit, safe, Catholic)

Personal intimacy (this is for YOU, not a system)

3. What should it feel like when they open the app?
FIRST FEELING (this matters a lot)

They should feel:

“Okay… I think I’m safe here.”

Then quickly:

“This is simple. I can do this.”

Then:

“This actually understands me.”

Then:

“Maybe this could help.”

4. What should it NOT feel like?

This is just as important.

❌ It should NOT feel like:

A theology course

A lecture

A church obligation

A diagnostic tool (“what’s wrong with you”)

A spiritual warfare manual (too early = fear)

Overwhelming or content-heavy

Judgmental or moralistic

❌ It should NOT trigger:

Shame

Panic

Scrupulosity

Information overload

5. The Emotional Arc (THIS IS GOLD)

Your whole program should move them like this:

Day 1–5

“I don’t understand what’s happening to me”

↓

Day 6–10

“Oh… this explains a lot”

↓

Day 11–15

“I see where I’ve gone wrong… but I’m not rejected”

↓

Day 16–20

“Jesus actually wants to help me”

↓

Day 21–25

“I think I can change”

↓

Day 26–30

“I want to follow Him”

6. One Sentence That Defines Your User

If I had to summarize your participant:

Someone who is wounded, searching, and spiritually disoriented, who is not looking for information — but for clarity, healing, and real encounter with God.

You are NOT building:

a 30-day course

a catechism app

a deliverance guide

You are building:

👉 A guided return to God for wounded people
