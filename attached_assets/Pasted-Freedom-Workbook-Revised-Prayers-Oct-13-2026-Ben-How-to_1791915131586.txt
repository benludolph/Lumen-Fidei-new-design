Freedom Workbook — Revised Prayers
Oct 13, 2026 · @Ben
How to read this
These three prayers replace prayer_1, prayer_2, and prayer_3. They draw only on the 17 retained sections and follow the same direction as the revised workbook: Christ's victory is the starting point, sin is repented, wounds are brought for healing, and every lie is answered with the truth.
Notes for the clergy reviewer
• Form of prayer. The participant renounces in the first person, which is an act of their own will. Every request for an evil spirit to be bound or removed is addressed to the Lord ("I ask You, Lord Jesus, to remove it"). The prayers contain no commands spoken directly to spirits, including on behalf of family members. The current daily prayer does command spirits over the participant's family; that language is removed.
• Family line. The participant forgives, intercedes, and renounces patterns in their own life. The prayer states that no one can repent on another's behalf or carry another's guilt.
• Prayer for the dead and invocation of saints appear only for Catholic participants. Non-Catholics entrust the dead to God's mercy and pray to God directly.
• Length. prayer_1 drops from 18 pages to 13, prayer_2 from 13 to 9, and prayer_3 from 14 to 7.
How the slots and conditions work
• [Words in square brackets] are filled from the participant's workbook. A list reads as "A, B, and C." A slot marked one per line shows each entry on its own line.
• Shown only if… under a page title means the whole page is skipped when that condition is not met. Participants never see an empty slot.
• [If Catholic: …] and [If not Catholic: …] depend on the participant's faith answer.
• [If any …: …] wraps a sentence that appears only when the participant filled in that optional field.
prayer_1: Personal Prayer for Freedom
Page 1: Opening
Father, I come to You as Your child. Jesus, You have already won the victory over sin, death, and every evil power, and I stand in that victory today. Holy Spirit, You are the one who sets me free. Lead me through this prayer.
I believe that You love me and that You see me as I am. I bring You what You have shown me, trusting that You want my freedom even more than I do.
I renounce Satan, all his works, and all his empty promises. I belong to Jesus Christ.
[If Catholic: Blessed Virgin Mary, pray for me. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend me. My guardian angel, stay close to me.]
Page 2: Turning back
Shown only if section 1 has entries.
Lord Jesus, I have turned away from You. I confess especially [sins from section 1]. These were wrong, and I am sorry. I do not want to defend them or hold on to them.
[If any "what I was looking for": When I did these things, I was looking for [what I was looking for, section 1]. You are the one who can give me what I was really looking for. Teach me to come to You first.]
[If any entry is marked "I encouraged, approved, or went along with it": Where I encouraged or went along with the sins of others, I withdraw my agreement.]
I renounce every hold these sins have had on me, and I ask You, Lord Jesus, to close every door I opened through them.
Page 3: Sexuality
Shown only if section 4 has entries.
Lord Jesus, You made my body and my sexuality good, and I have used them apart from love. I confess especially [sins from section 4]. I am sorry. Restore my purity and my dignity.
[If any person connected: I bring before You [persons connected, section 4]. I release each of them to You and ask You to bless them. I renounce every unholy bond formed between us, and I ask You to set us both free.]
I renounce lust and every evil spirit that has used these sins to hold me, and I ask You, Lord Jesus, to remove them from me. Heal my memory and imagination, and give me a chaste and generous heart.
Page 4: What I run to
Shown only if section 11 has entries.
Father, when I am hurting I have run to other things before running to You. I have run to [what I run to, section 11].
[If any "what it gives me": They gave me [what it gives me, section 11]. I want to receive what I truly need from You.]
I renounce the false comfort and false security these have offered me. Where they have become a bondage, I ask You, Lord Jesus, to break their hold and remove every evil spirit that has used them against me. Give me the help I need from others to walk free, and the humility to accept it.
Page 5: People I have hurt
Shown only if section 13 has entries.
Lord Jesus, I have hurt people You love. I bring before You [harm done, section 13]. I am sorry. Heal what I damaged, and bless each person I hurt.
[If any marked "I may owe an apology or restitution": Show me how to make amends where I owe them, and give me the courage and prudence to do it well.]
Page 6: Seeking power apart from God
Shown only if section 5 or section 20 has entries.
[If section 5 has entries: Father, I looked for guidance, protection, and power apart from You. I repent of it, and I renounce [practices, section 5].]
[If any spirit named, one per line: I renounce [spirit named]. I will not serve you. Jesus Christ is my only Lord.]
[If any entry is marked "A spell, curse, or pact I made": I renounce every spell, curse, and pact I made, and I take back every consent I gave.]
[If section 5 has entries: I ask You, Lord Jesus, to break every bond these practices formed, to remove every evil spirit connected with them, and to fill those places with Your Holy Spirit.]
[If section 20 has entries: I will remove from my home [objects, section 20]. Let nothing in my home stand for anyone but You.]
Page 7: Lies and the truth
Shown only if section 6 has entries.
Father, I have believed things about myself, about You, and about others that are not true. I renounce these lies: [lies, section 6].
[One per line, for each lie with a truth: I reject the lie that [lie]. The Lord reminds me: [truth]. As Scripture says, [reference]: "[verse]."]
I break my agreement with every lie spoken to me through other people, through my own pain, or by the enemy. I choose to stand on Your word.
Page 8: Vows
Shown only if section 7 has entries.
Lord Jesus, I made promises to protect myself: [vows, section 7]. I made them in pain, and they have ruled parts of my life that belong to You. I renounce them. I give back to You the places in my heart I tried to guard on my own. Protect them Yourself.
Page 9: Bonds that still hold me
Shown only if section 24 has entries.
Father, some bonds still pull at me. I bring before You [persons, section 24]. I release each person into Your hands and I bless them. I renounce every unholy bond between us, and I ask You, Lord Jesus, to cut each one and to restore to each of us what belongs to us. Set my love in order.
Page 10: Wounds
Shown only if section 3 has entries.
Jesus, You were with me when I was hurt. I bring You these wounds: [what happened, section 3]. What was done to me was not my fault, and I place it in Your hands.
[If any "message I took from it": Because of these wounds I believed [messages, section 3]. I let go of those messages and ask You to tell me what is true.]
[If any "how I learned to protect myself": I learned to protect myself by [ways I protected myself, section 3]. Thank You for carrying me through. I give these ways to You now. Where they led me into sin, forgive me, and teach me to trust You instead.]
Come into these memories, Lord, and heal what is still wounded.
[If any "what the Lord is showing me": Thank You for showing me [what the Lord is showing me, section 3].]
Page 11: Releasing
Shown only if section 2 has entries.
[If any entries are "Someone who hurt me" or unmarked: Father, I choose to forgive. One per line: I forgive [person] for [what happened]. I release them from the debt they owe me, place them in Your hands, and ask You to bless them. Where my feelings have not caught up, I trust You to heal my heart over time. I renounce bitterness, resentment, and the desire for revenge.]
[If any entry is "Myself": Father, I have held my own failures against myself, especially [what happened, "Myself" entries]. You have forgiven me. I receive Your forgiveness, and I stop condemning what You have already forgiven.]
[If any entry is "My anger at God": Father, I have been angry with You about [what happened, "My anger at God" entries]. I tell You so honestly. I let go of my accusation against You, and I ask You to show me Your heart in what happened.]
Page 12: The thread
Shown only if section 14 has checked themes.
Father, I see these threads running through my life: [checked themes, section 14]. I renounce them. Wherever an evil spirit has used them, I renounce it in the name of Jesus Christ, and I ask You, Lord Jesus, to remove it from me and leave it no place to return.
[If "what I most need to hear" is filled: Father, speak this over me: [what I most need to hear, section 14]. I receive it.]
Page 13: Closing
Holy Spirit, fill every place in me that has been emptied today. Where there was a lie, put Your truth. Where there was a wound, put Your healing. Where there was fear, put Your peace. Leave no room in me for anything that is not from You.
[If Catholic: I will bring my sins to Confession and receive Your mercy there. Blessed Virgin Mary, cover me with your mantle. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend me. All you holy saints of God, pray for me.]
[If not Catholic: Give me the courage to confess my sins to a pastor or a believer I trust, so that I may be healed.]
Jesus Christ is Lord. I belong to Him, and I rest in His victory. Amen.
prayer_2: Family Line Prayer
Page 1: Standing for my family
Father, I come before You for my family: my father's side and my mother's side, those who came before me and those who will come after me.
I am not here to judge anyone. I cannot repent on another person's behalf, and I do not carry their guilt. I come to forgive them, to pray for them, and to refuse to carry forward anything that did not come from You.
Jesus, You are Lord of my family's past, present, and future. Cover us with Your mercy.
Page 2: Patterns in my family line
Shown only if section 15 has entries.
Father, these patterns have run through my family:
[One per line: [pattern], with "(father's side)", "(mother's side)", "(both sides)", or "(adoptive family)" added when marked.]
I forgive those whose choices began these patterns or passed them on. I renounce these patterns in my own life, and I ask You to let them end with me.
Page 3: Family members of concern
Shown only if section 16 has entries.
Father, I bring before You:
[One per line: [who], followed by " — [what concerns me]" when filled.]
I forgive them, and I entrust each of them to Your mercy.
[If any group named: I renounce every oath, ritual, and dedication made through [groups, section 16], as far as they touch me and my family, and I ask You to break their hold on us.]
Page 4: The family dead
Shown only if section 25 has entries.
Father of mercy, I bring before You the members of my family who died in sorrow, in violence, or without prayer.
[One per line. For a miscarried or aborted child: I entrust this child, [name given now, or "known to You by name"], to Your mercy. I acknowledge this child as part of our family, and I mourn this child now. Receive this child into Your arms. For everyone else: I remember [name] before You. I mourn this loss, and I entrust [name] to Your mercy.]
[If Catholic: Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May their souls and the souls of all the faithful departed, through the mercy of God, rest in peace.]
[If not Catholic: I place each of them in Your hands. You are the Judge of all, and You are rich in mercy.]
I ask that no grief or fear connected with their deaths continue in our family.
Page 5: Forgiving those who harmed my family
Shown only if any section 2 entry is marked "This affected my whole family."
Father, I choose to forgive those whose sins wounded my whole family:
[One per line: I forgive [person] for [what happened].]
I release my judgment against them and place them in Your hands.
Page 6: Body and mind
Shown only if section 22 has entries.
Father, I bring before You what I carry in body and mind, and what my family has carried: [infirmities, section 22]. Where any of this is connected with sin or with the patterns of my family, break that connection.
[If any marked for healing: According to Your will, I ask You to heal [infirmities marked for healing].]
Bless the doctors and everyone who cares for me.
Page 7: What ends with me
Shown only if section 23 has entries.
Father, let these end with me:
[One per line: [what ends with me], exactly as the participant wrote it.]
[If any blessing filled: For those who come after me, I ask: [blessings, section 23, one per line].]
Page 8: Blessing over my family
Father, where my family has known curses, give blessing. Where there has been sickness, give healing. Where there has been unbelief, give faith. Where there has been violence, give peace. Where there has been abandonment, give faithfulness.
Holy Spirit, fill my family.
[If Catholic: Blessed Virgin Mary, spread your mantle over this family. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us. Holy angels of God, guard our homes and our children.]
Jesus Christ, reign in my family. Thank You for Your mercy toward me and toward all of us. Amen.
Page 9: Bringing this further
[If Catholic: Bring these intentions to Mass and offer them silently at the Offertory, especially the members of your family you named among the dead. Any parish can arrange for a Mass to be offered for the deceased of your family. Return to this prayer as often as the Lord leads you.]
[If not Catholic: Bring these intentions with you to worship, and ask your pastor or the ministry team to pray with you. Return to this prayer as often as the Lord leads you.]
prayer_3: Daily Prayer
Page 1: Opening
Father, I begin this day as Your child. Jesus, You are Lord over today and over everything I will face. Holy Spirit, fill me and lead me.
I belong to You. I do not need to earn Your love today. Help me live from it.
Page 2: Turning and releasing
Lord, since I last prayed, I have turned from You in these ways: [Day-of input, not saved: "Anything you want to name today?"]. I am sorry. Forgive me and make me new.
[If Catholic: Lead me to Confession where I need it.]
I forgive [Day-of input, not saved: "Anyone you need to forgive today?"]. I release them to You and ask You to bless them.
I renounce [Day-of input, not saved: "Anything pulling at you today?"] in the name of Jesus Christ, and I ask You, Lord, to take it from me.
Page 3: The truth I stand on
Father, I reject every lie that has shaped the way I see myself, and I receive what You say is true.
[One per line, for each lie with a truth: I reject the lie that [lie]. The Lord reminds me: [truth]. As Scripture says, [reference]: "[verse]."]
[If no lies have truths yet: Father, I receive Your word over me today. I am Your beloved child. You see me. Nothing can separate me from Your love.]
Let these truths go deeper than my feelings today.
Page 4: The thread
Shown only if section 14 has checked themes.
Lord Jesus, I renounce [checked themes, section 14] again today. Keep them from having any place in me.
[If "what I most need to hear" is filled: Father, remind me today: [what I most need to hear, section 14].]
Page 5: My family and home
[If family members are listed: Father, I place my family in Your hands, especially [family members].]
[If spouse is listed: Bless [spouse]. Draw [spouse] close to You, heal what is wounded, and keep us faithful to each other.]
[If children are listed: Bless [children]. Protect them, strengthen their faith, and keep them from evil.]
Lord Jesus, protect my family and my home from every evil. Let my home be a place of peace and prayer.
[If Catholic: Blessed Virgin Mary, cover us with your mantle. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us. Holy angels of God, guard us.]
Page 6: Healing
[If chronic issues are listed: Lord Jesus, I bring You what I carry: [chronic issues].]
[If identity labels are listed: I let go of the name [identity labels]. My name is Your beloved.]
[If any section 22 entries are marked for healing: According to Your will, heal [infirmities marked for healing].]
Heal my mind, my memory, and my imagination today. Where I need human help, give me the courage to receive it.
Page 7: Closing
Holy Spirit, bring forth Your fruit in me today: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.
Jesus Christ, reign in my heart, my home, and my family today. Deliver us from evil. Amen.
