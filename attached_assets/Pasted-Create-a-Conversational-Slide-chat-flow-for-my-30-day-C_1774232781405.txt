Create a Conversational Slide (chat_flow) for my 30-day Catholic formation app.

This is a guided, branching reflection module (8–12 minutes) designed to help users:

recognize patterns in their life
identify underlying beliefs (lies/agreements)
begin seeing their story through Christ’s perspective

This is NOT AI chat — it is a scripted, state-driven flow.

🧩 MODULE NAME

“The Origin of the Weight”

🔁 FLOW STRUCTURE (STEPS)
STEP 1 — Identify the Weight

Prompt:
“What has the strongest hold on you right now?”

Input: single select

Options:

Shame
Anxiety
Anger
Control
Rejection
Guilt

Store as: emotion

STEP 2 — When Does This Show Up

System message 1:
“When you think about moments where you’ve felt {{emotion}}, what comes to mind first?”

System message 2 (immediately after):
“This could be something that happens often… or a moment from a long time ago.”

Input: single select

Options:

Something from a long time ago
Something more recent
Something happening right now
Nothing specific comes to mind
This doesn’t really fit me

Store as: time_context

STEP 3 — Branch Based on Time Context
IF: long time ago OR recent

Prompt:
“What stands out most about that moment?”

Input: single select + optional text

Options:

What someone did or said
How I felt
What I did
I’m not sure
(Optional) “Describe briefly” (text)

Store as: memory_focus

IF: happening right now

Prompt:
“What is it doing to you right now?”

Input: single select

Options:

It feels overwhelming
It keeps repeating in my mind
I feel stuck
I feel numb
Other

Store as: present_effect

IF: nothing comes to mind

System message:
“That’s okay. Sometimes these patterns form over time, not from one single moment.”

👉 Continue forward (do NOT block user)

IF: “this doesn’t fit me”

👉 Return to Step 1

STEP 4 — The Belief (CORE STEP)

Prompt:
“In that moment… what did you start to believe?”

Input: single select + optional text

👉 Options should be dynamically tied to emotion

Example (Anxiety):

I’m not safe
Something bad is going to happen
I need to stay in control
I can’t trust others
Other (text)

Example (Shame):

Something is wrong with me
I’m not enough
I’m unlovable
I will always fail
Other (text)

Store as: core_belief

STEP 5 — Present Impact

Prompt:
“How does this affect you now?”

Input: multi-select

Options:

Avoiding people
Overthinking
Fear or anxiety
Anger reactions
Numbness / shutdown
Trying to control everything
Other

Store as: current_patterns

STEP 6 — Compassion Pivot (Bridge Step)

Prompt:
“If you saw someone else go through that… what would you think about them?”

Input: single select

Options:

They didn’t deserve that
They were trying their best
They needed help
They were alone
I’m not sure

Store as: compassion_response

STEP 7 — Entering the Scene (Christ Introduction)

System message:
“Now bring Jesus into that moment.”

Follow-up:
“In your mind, where is He?”

Input: single select + optional text

Options:

Standing near me
Watching over me
Reaching toward me
I don’t see Him
I’m not sure
Other (text)

Store as: jesus_position

STEP 8 — Perspective Shift (YOUR REWRITE — KEY STEP)

Prompt (exact tone requested):

“Now step into His perspective.

Into the scene of that moment in your life.

Look at yourself through His eyes.

What does He see?
How does He feel about you or your choices at the time?
What is His perspective on what happened as a whole?”

Input:

Optional text (encouraged but not required)

Also include guided options (important):

He sees someone who was hurting
He sees someone who needed help
He is not condemning me
He is still present there
I’m not sure

Store as: christ_perspective

STEP 9 — Integration

Prompt:
“When this feeling shows up again, what will you do?”

Input: single select

Options:

Pause and recognize it
Remember what is actually true
Invite Jesus into the moment
Reach out to someone
I’m not sure yet

Store as: response_plan

STEP 10 — FINAL SUMMARY CARD

Display a dynamic summary using stored variables

Title:
“What You’ve Seen”

Body:
“This pattern didn’t begin today.

It formed when a belief took hold:
‘{{core_belief}}’

And it still affects you through:
{{current_patterns}}

But that is not the full story.

In that moment, Jesus was not absent.
He was {{jesus_position}}.

And from His perspective:
{{christ_perspective}}

You are not defined by {{emotion}}.
You are seen, known, and not abandoned.”
