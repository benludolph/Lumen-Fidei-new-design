I want to create a new slide type called “Interactive Story Slide.”

This slide will contain a visual glowing orb that represents the heart/soul and the user progresses through a story by clicking buttons.

Important:
Use the exact wording I provide. Do NOT rewrite the text.
The focus is the visual interaction with the orb.

Orb Design

Create a soft glowing orb in the center of the screen.

The orb should:

• glow gold / warm light
• slowly pulse like breathing
• feel alive and gentle
• have a subtle light aura around it

The pulsing animation should:

• expand slightly
• increase brightness
• then contract
• cycle every 3–4 seconds

It should feel calm and spiritual, not like a video game.

Orb States

The orb will change appearance as the story progresses.

States include:

State 1 — Pure Light
Bright glowing orb with soft pulse.

State 2 — Crack
A thin jagged black crack appears inside the orb.
The glow flickers slightly.

State 3 — Shell
A gray protective shell forms around the orb's edge.

State 4 — Scribbles
Dark scribbles and marks appear over the orb.

Important:
The golden light should always remain faintly visible behind the damage.

Interaction

The story progresses through stages inside the slide.

Each stage includes:

• text narration
• orb visual change
• button to continue

Example:

Stage 1
Orb bright and glowing

Text:
“Hi [NAME]!
I’m your soul as a child!
I’m excited for life! I’m hopeful for the future!
I wonder what life has in store for me!”

Button: Step into the world

Stage 2
Small crack appears

Text:
“Something happened today.
Someone I loved said something mean.
It felt like a cold wind blew through me.”

Button: Keep going

Stage 3
Shell forms

Text:
“To make sure that doesn't happen again,
I built a little wall.”

Button: Try to be strong

Stage 4
Orb dim with scribbles

Text:
“Now I’m older.
I’ve been hurt, so I hurt people back.”

Button: Keep going

Stage 5
User presses Try Harder

Each click shakes the orb slightly but does not remove the scribbles.

After several clicks show message:

“I tried to fix myself…
but I couldn’t reach the light anymore.”

Then show Continue button.

Integration

This interactive story must behave like a single slide in the lesson system.

When the story finishes, the normal Next Slide button should appear.

Do not modify the existing lesson engine.
Only add a new slide type and renderer.
