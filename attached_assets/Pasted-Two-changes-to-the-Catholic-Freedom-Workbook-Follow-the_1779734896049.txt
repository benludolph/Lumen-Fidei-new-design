Page — Soul ties and unhealthy bonds
Lord Jesus Christ, I bring before You every unhealthy bond and unholy soul tie that has formed between me and another person — bonds of the heart, the mind, the will, the emotions, or the body that hold me apart from Your freedom. I bring before You especially {{INJECT: Section 24, format=names_comma using person_or_relationship}}.
I have already chosen to forgive those who wounded me. Now I ask You to set me free from every cord that still binds me to them. I renounce every soul tie formed through sexual sin, abuse, control, manipulation, fear, trauma, rejection, dependency, idolatry of another person, addiction, grief that would not release the dead, or any unholy agreement, ritual, or covenant — whether I formed it knowingly or unknowingly, whether I remember it or have forgotten it.
In the holy name of Jesus, and with the sword of His Cross, I sever and break every tie, bond, and attachment I have to these persons, and every tie, bond, and attachment they have to me. I return to them everything I took that was not mine to take, and I reclaim from them everything that was taken from me that was not theirs to take. I reject every spirit that has used these bonds to influence me, control me, drain me, deceive me, or keep me from belonging wholly to God.
Through the power of the Precious Blood of Jesus, I ask Almighty God to bind every evil spirit connected to these soul ties, to break its power over me, to cast it away from me, and to wash both me and every person I have named in the Precious Blood of Christ. Lord Jesus, gather back every fragmented part of my soul, restore me whole in You, and let me belong fully to You alone.
