Spec: Fix Generational Renunciation Prayer (Prayer 2) + Workbook Changes

For implementation in the Lumen Fidei app. Two parts: (A) workbook schema changes, (B) the new Prayer 2 template with dynamic insertion + skip rules.

Design goals (based on Healing the Whole Person / Christian Healing Ministries generational model):


Add the representative stance (pray-er stands in for the whole family line, both sides, past and future).
Add a Family Dead section — the biggest current gap (suicides, tragic deaths, miscarried/aborted children, the unmourned, those without Christian burial). The prayer must pray FOR these people, not only renounce effects.
Order the prayer to the Mass — end with a hand-off directing the participant to offer these intentions at Mass (this supplies the priest, since none is present in-program).
Compress: boilerplate once at open and close; each middle section = 3–5 sentences around the entries; skip empty sections. Target 10–15 min read-aloud vs. current ~40.
De-dup with Prayer 1: everything personal stays in Prayer 1. Prayer 2 = ancestral/inherited/family only.



PART A — Workbook Changes

A1. NEW Section 25 — "The Family Dead" [Category: Generational Lines]

Description (participant-facing):
"Some members of a family line die in ways that leave a wound behind them — suicide, violence, accident, war. Others die and are never truly mourned: children lost to miscarriage or abortion who were never named, relatives who died alone in institutions, those who never received a Christian burial or any prayer at all. The Church has always prayed for the dead. In this section you identify the members of your family line — known or unknown, named or unnamed — who need to be mourned, entrusted to God's mercy, and prayed for, so that grief and affliction do not continue down the line unaddressed."

Action Item:
"Go through your family line on both sides, as far back as you know. List by name (or by relationship, if the name is unknown) family members who: took their own lives or attempted to; were murdered or died tragically (accidents, war); died untimely or young; died alone, unloved, or in institutions; were never given a Christian burial or never mourned; and every child lost to miscarriage or abortion in your family line — including your own. If a child was never named, you may give them a name now."

Fields:


name (text) — name or relationship ("My grandmother's brother")
circumstance (single-select): Suicide or attempted suicide / Murdered or violent death / Tragic accident or war / Untimely or early death / Died alone, unloved, or in an institution / Never mourned or no Christian burial / Miscarried child / Aborted child / Other
notes (textarea, optional)
named_now (text, optional) — "If this child was never named, what name do you give them?"


A2. Section 15 — restructure prompts (no schema change required, copy change)

Update the action item to walk the HWP family-tree method: "Map both sides of your family, going back at least four generations, even where you don't know names. Patterns pass down vertically (grandfather → father) and sideways (aunt to aunt, cousin to cousin). If you were adopted, include what you know of your biological family AND your adoptive family — both lines affect you. Don't worry about what you don't know; name what you can and trust God with the rest."

Optional enhancement: add a side select (Father's side / Mother's side / Both / Adoptive family) to each Section 15 entry so the prayer can group them.

A3. Program tie-in (content change, not schema)


Day 13 (confession day): instruct participants to bring Prayer 1 material (Sections 1, 4, 9, 11, 13) to Confession.
Day 27 (Mass day): instruct participants to bring their Prayer 2 list — especially Section 25, the Family Dead — and offer it at Mass; suggest requesting a Mass intention for the deceased of their family line.

Comprehensive Binding and Blocking (always included — full catalog appears here once)

Father, in the Name of Jesus Christ and by the power of His Precious Blood, I now bring before You everything I have renounced in this prayer, together with everything hidden, forgotten, or unknown in our family line.

I ask You to bind and break every curse, pact, spell, hex, seal, oath, vow, ritual bond, blood covenant, malefice, assignment, ancestral curse, and every other demonic bondage operating in our family line — and to cast out every evil spirit that has claimed access to this family through any of it.

I ask You to block every one of these evils from passing to my children, my descendants, and all future generations, especially:


[SECTION 23 ENTRIES — "Let ___ end with me."]



Let the Precious Blood of Jesus stand between my family and every evil inheritance. Let these evils end now, in this generation, through Your infinite mercy and the power of the Holy Cross.


Blessing over the Line (always included — the prayer ends on blessing)

Father, I now ask for blessings where there have been curses, and healing where there has been sickness — physical, psychological, or spiritual — throughout our whole family line.

Bless my ancestors, living and dead, according to Your mercy. Bless my children and descendants; let them inherit faith where there was unbelief, peace where there was violence, fidelity where there was abandonment, and life where there was death.

Holy Spirit, fill every place in our family line that has been emptied of evil. Blessed Virgin Mary, spread your mantle over this family. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us. Holy angels of God, surround our homes and our children.

Lord Jesus Christ, reign in this family — in our past, our present, and our future. Thank You for Your mercy and Your kindness toward me and all my family. Amen.


Bringing This to the Mass (always included — closing instruction, not prayer text)

This prayer is completed at the altar. Bring these intentions — especially the members of your family line you named among the Family Dead — to Mass, and offer them silently with the bread and wine at the Offertory. If you are able, request a Mass to be offered for the deceased of your family line; any parish can arrange this. Generational healing may need to be prayed more than once — return to this prayer, and to the Mass, as often as the Lord leads you.
