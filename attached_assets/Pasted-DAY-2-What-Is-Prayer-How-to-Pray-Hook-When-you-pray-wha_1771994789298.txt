DAY 2 – What Is Prayer? How to Pray

Hook
When you pray, what usually happens?

☐ I don’t know what to say

☐ I get distracted

☐ I feel awkward

☐ I only pray when I’m stressed

☐ I avoid prayer altogether

Teach
Prayer is not a technique to get outcomes.
It’s learning how to be present to God honestly.
Most people think they’re bad at prayer when really they were never taught a simple way to show up.
Prayer grows through consistency, not emotional intensity.

Video
(Optional) “What is prayer?” video

Activity
Pick one prayer posture you’ll try this week:

☐ 60 seconds of silence

☐ One honest sentence to God

☐ Reading one Psalm

☐ The Our Father slowly

Scripture
Philippians 4:6
Prayer is about bringing your real life to God, not hiding it.

Practice
One honest sentence prayer right now.

Close
Prayer is relationship, not performance.

DAY 3 – Is God Real? (Maybe You’re Skeptical… That’s Okay)

Hook
Does God feel real in your daily life?
(0 = not at all, 10 = very real)

Teach
Doubt doesn’t mean you’re broken.
It often means your lived experience doesn’t match what you’ve been told about God.
Christian faith doesn’t begin with emotional certainty—it begins with honest seeking.
God is not a force to prove Himself on demand. He is a Person who invites relationship over time.

Video
(Optional) Faith vs superstition video

Activity
Interactive: Control-based vs relationship-based faith checklist

Scripture
Mark 9:24 – “I believe; help my unbelief.”
Honest doubt is part of real faith.

Practice
One-minute experiment:
“God, if you’re real, I’m open to taking one honest step today.”

Close
Faith starts with seeking, not certainty.

DAY 4 – What Is Your Image of God? Tackling Lies

Hook
When you think about God, which word comes first?

☐ Loving

☐ Distant

☐ Judgmental

☐ Disappointed

☐ Unknown

Teach
Most people don’t struggle with God Himself—they struggle with their image of God.
Your image of God is shaped by parents, authority figures, wounds, and past experiences.
If your image of God is distorted, your prayer life will feel unsafe or forced.

Video
(Optional) Short teaching on distorted images of God

Activity
Spiritual inventory: “Which image of God did I grow up with?”

Scripture
1 John 4:8 – God is love
Love means presence and faithfulness, not permissiveness.

Practice
Ask God: “Show me where my image of You may be distorted.”

Close
You can’t relate to God rightly if you’re relating to a false image of Him.

DAY 5 – Spiritual Warfare Basics: Naming Lies vs Truth

Hook
Which inner voice shows up most when you’re stressed?

☐ “I’m failing.”

☐ “God left me.”

☐ “This will never change.”

☐ “I’m unsafe.”

☐ “I’m alone.”

Teach
Not every thought you have is true.
Fear, shame, and accusation distort how you see God and yourself.
Spiritual maturity includes learning how to pause, name lies, and return to truth.

Video
(Optional) Teaching on lies vs truth

Activity
Name one lie you believe → choose a truth to counter it.

Scripture
John 10:10
God’s voice leads toward life, not fear.

Practice
When a negative thought arises today, pause and ask: “Is this drawing me toward truth or fear?”

Close
Discernment is about clarity, not paranoia.

DAY 6 – God Loves You: Made for Relationship

Hook
Do you believe God loves you personally, or only “in general”?

Teach
Christianity is not primarily about rules.
It’s about relationship.
God does not love an abstract version of you.
He loves you personally, in your real life, with your real struggles.

Video
(Optional) Song: Good Good Father

Activity
Reflection: What makes it hard to believe God loves you personally?

Scripture
John 3:16
God’s love is directed at persons, not crowds.

Practice
Sit quietly for 60 seconds and let this sentence be true:
“God loves me.”

Close
God’s love is not theoretical—it’s personal.

DAY 7 – We Are His Children: Receiving a New Identity

Hook
Which identity do you live from most often?

☐ I’m unworthy

☐ I’m on my own

☐ I’m a problem

☐ I’m loved

☐ I’m unsure

Teach
Christian faith doesn’t just change what you believe.
It changes who you understand yourself to be.
You are not defined by fear, shame, or your past.
You are invited into the identity of being God’s child.

Video
(Optional) Song: No Longer Slaves

Activity
Choose one false identity you want to let go of this week.

Scripture
1 John 4:8–9
God’s love gives you a new identity.

Practice
One-minute prayer:
“God, help me learn to live as Your child, not from fear.”

Close
This week wasn’t about mastering ideas.
It was about reorienting your relationship with God.
