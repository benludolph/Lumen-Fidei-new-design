Purpose of this page

How recipients consent to text messages
This page documents the complete opt-in process for SMS messages sent by the Archdiocese of Chicago Spiritual Healing Ministry. Consent is collected on the ministry's official online intake form, which displays the Archdiocese of Chicago name and crest at the top and bottom of every page.

A person provides their phone number on the form, selects “SMS text message” as their preferred contact method, reads the consent agreement, and checks the consent box to agree. Text messages are limited to appointment reminders and scheduling updates related to the person's own ministry request. SMS consent is never required to receive ministry services — every recipient may choose email instead.

Consent language, in full

The agreement each recipient accepts
“I agree to receive SMS text messages from the Archdiocese of Chicago Healing Ministry at the phone number I provided. These text messages may include appointment reminders and scheduling updates related to my ministry request. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. I understand that SMS consent is not required to receive ministry services. I may choose email instead.”
