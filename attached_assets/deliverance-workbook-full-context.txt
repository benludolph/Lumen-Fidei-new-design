================================================================================
LUMEN FIDEI — DELIVERANCE WORKBOOK: FULL CONTEXT
================================================================================

WHAT IS THE DELIVERANCE WORKBOOK?

The Deliverance Workbook is a structured spiritual inventory that participants
complete during the Lumen Fidei 30-day program (Track A). It is organized into
5 categories and 24 sections. Each section asks the participant to list specific
things — sins, wounds, people, vows, patterns — that will be addressed in prayer
during a deliverance session (typically with a priest or trained minister).

Once the workbook is completed, it generates three personalized Catholic
deliverance prayers that weave the participant's specific entries into verbatim
prayer text:

  • Prayer 1 — Personal Deliverance Prayer (renounces personal sins, wounds,
               occult involvement, vows, soul ties, etc.)
  • Prayer 2 — Generational Renunciation Prayer (renounces ancestral sin
               patterns, secret societies, blood contracts, generational curses)
  • Prayer 3 — Daily Prayer (an ongoing daily protection and healing prayer)

The prayers are paginated (17, 17, and 14 pages respectively) and are designed
to be prayed aloud, ideally with a priest present. The participant's workbook
entries are dynamically inserted into the prayer text at the appropriate places
(e.g., their list of people to forgive is inserted into the forgiveness page).

================================================================================
SECTION CATEGORIES
================================================================================

1. Personal Sins
   Sin, brokenness, and the patterns that give the enemy ground in your life.

2. Occult & Dark Powers
   Involvement with occult practices, false religions, and spiritual powers that
   are not from God.

3. Generational Lines
   Sins, patterns, and bondages inherited from your family line.

4. Inner Vows
   The lies you have believed and the vows you have made that quietly govern
   your life.

5. Wounds & Forgiveness
   The wounds you have received, the people you need to forgive, and the trauma
   that shaped you.

================================================================================
THE 24 WORKBOOK SECTIONS
================================================================================

SECTION 1 — Personal Sins  [Category: Personal Sins]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Use this section for personal sins that don't belong in one of the specialized
sections that follow — things like pride, anger, envy, gossip, dishonesty,
neglected prayer, unkindness, and the ordinary failures of daily life. Sexual
sin, occult involvement, idolatry, vows, addictions, and soul ties each have
their own dedicated section later. Bring only what doesn't fit there. Nothing
you name here is too large for mercy, and nothing is too small to matter.

Action Item:
Ask the Holy Spirit to surface the personal sins you've committed in thought,
word, deed, or omission that belong here. Write each one plainly — no
explanations, no minimizing. One entry per sin. These are what you'll bring to
the priest in Confession.

Fields: Sin (textarea)


SECTION 2 — People to Forgive  [Category: Wounds & Forgiveness]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Unforgiveness often leads to significant torment in people's lives. Forgiveness
is a choice rather than an emotion. It involves choosing to forgive someone who
has harmed you. Forgiveness does not require you to reconcile with that person
nor allow that person back into your life. It is a one-sided action that you
do. Forgiveness does not mean you agree with what that person did to you. It
merely means you release your judgment against that person and free them from
your heart so you can be freed from basanizo torment (Matthew 18:34–35).

Action Item:
Make a list of everyone who has hurt you — either directly against you or
indirectly against a loved one — whether they are still living or dead (include
yourself if you have unforgiveness toward yourself, and include God if you have
held resentment toward Him). Then list every hurt they did to you. You don't
need to soften or explain what happened. Simply name it honestly and move on.
Examples: My father, for the way he spoke to me. My ex-spouse, for the affair.
The driver who killed my brother. Myself, for the abortion I can't forgive.

Fields: Person (text), What happened — optional (textarea),
        "This affected my whole family, not just me" (yes/no)


SECTION 3 — Family & Childhood Wounds  [Category: Wounds & Forgiveness]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
The home is where the heart is first shaped, and it is also where the deepest
wounds are often inflicted — not always through cruelty, but sometimes through
absence, neglect, favoritism, or words spoken in a moment of anger that lodged
in a child's soul for decades. These early wounds form the lens through which
you still see yourself, God, and authority. Bringing them into the light is not
about blaming your family; it is about letting Jesus heal the boy or girl who
first received the wound.

Action Item:
Go back through your childhood and adolescence and write down the moments that
still sting — the things said over you, the comparisons, the time you were
overlooked, the home that was too loud or too silent. Note the wound and the
message you took from it. Examples: "You'll never amount to anything" → I am a
failure. Being left out at school → I don't belong. A parent's coldness → I am
unlovable.

Fields: Source (Father / Mother / Family or Childhood), What happened (textarea),
        Lie I believed because of this — optional (textarea)


SECTION 4 — Sexual Sin  [Category: Personal Sins]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Sexuality is meant to be a sacred bond, and precisely because it is so powerful,
sin in this area creates ties that linger long after the act is over.
Pornography, fornication, adultery, fantasy, and abuse all leave imprints — soul
ties to past partners, shame that whispers you are "damaged," and patterns that
demand to be fed. God is neither shocked nor disgusted by what you bring here.
He longs to break every ungodly tie and restore your purity and dignity.

Action Item:
With honesty and without graphic detail, write down areas of sexual sin and
brokenness — relationships, habits, and exposures, including those done to you
that left a stain you've carried as guilt. List people you have been sexually
involved with, by name where possible, so that soul ties can be named and
severed.

Fields: Sin/description (textarea), Person connected — optional (text)


SECTION 5 — Occult & False Spiritual Power  [Category: Occult & Dark Powers]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
There is real power in the spiritual world, and not all of it comes from God.
Any attempt to gain knowledge, control, or protection apart from Him — however
curious, harmless, or "just for fun" it seemed — opens a door to the enemy.
This includes practices many treat as entertainment. The point is not fear but
renunciation: you are closing doors you may have opened without realizing where
they led.

Action Item:
List every occult or false-power practice you have engaged with, even once,
even as a child or "as a joke." Examples: Ouija boards, tarot or fortune-telling,
psychics and mediums, horoscopes, séances, witchcraft or spells, Reiki and
energy healing, New Age channeling, charms and amulets, blood pacts, summoning
games. Note who introduced you to it and roughly when.

Fields: Practice or involvement (text)


SECTION 6 — Lies & Truths  [Category: Inner Vows]
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
The enemy is called the father of lies, and his strategy is rarely a single
dramatic deception — it is a quiet sentence about you that you came to accept
as fact. These lies become the agreements that govern your life: "I am worthless,"
"God could never love someone like me," "I have to earn my place." Freedom comes
when each lie is renounced and replaced with what God actually says is true.
Truth is not a feeling; it is a foundation you choose to stand on.

Action Item:
Write down the lies you have believed about yourself, about God, and about
others. Beside each one, write the truth from Scripture or the heart of God
that replaces it. Examples: Lie: "I am a mistake." → Truth: "I am fearfully and
wonderfully made (Ps 139:14)." Lie: "I have to be perfect to be loved." → Truth:
"While I was still a sinner, Christ died for me (Rom 5:8)."

Fields: Each entry has a Lie field, a "Who told you this lie?" field (optional),
        and a Truth field.


SECTION 7 — Vows & Inner Promises  [Category: Inner Vows]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
In moments of deep pain we often make silent vows — "I will never let anyone
hurt me again," "I will never cry," "I will never be like my mother," "I will
never trust again." These promises feel like protection, but they hand control
of our hearts to the wound rather than to God. An inner vow can quietly steer
decades of decisions, walling off the very places that need healing. Renouncing
them returns the throne of your heart to Christ.

Action Item:
Recall the vows and inner promises you have made, especially in seasons of hurt,
fear, or anger. They often begin with "I will always…" or "I will never…". Write
each one out. Examples: "I'll never depend on anyone." "I'll never let them see
me weak." "I'll never forgive him." "I'll always be the strong one."

Fields: Vow or inner promise (textarea)


SECTION 8 — Idolatry  [Category: Personal Sins]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
An idol is anything that takes the place that belongs to God alone — the thing
you run to for security, comfort, identity, or worth. Idols are rarely golden
statues; they are usually good things loved in a disordered way: career, money,
a relationship, approval, control, comfort, even ministry. The test is simple:
what would devastate you to lose, more than losing God? Naming an idol is not
about contempt for the thing, but about restoring God to first place.

Action Item:
Ask yourself where you have placed your trust, identity, or ultimate hope
outside of God, and write each one down. Examples: My career and reputation.
Money and security. My children's approval. A romantic relationship. Control
over my life. Food or comfort. The opinions of others. Be honest about which
of these you serve more than God.

Fields: Idol or false source (textarea)


SECTION 9 — Pride, Rebellion & Control  [Category: Personal Sins]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
At the root of the first sin was a refusal to let God be God — a desire to
decide good and evil for ourselves. Pride, rebellion, and control are that same
root, grown into the everyday habits of needing to be right, resenting authority,
manipulating outcomes, and refusing to submit. They masquerade as strength but
leave us isolated and unable to receive. Humility is not weakness; it is the
soil in which every other grace can grow.

Action Item:
Write down the ways pride, rebellion, and control show up in your life — toward
God, toward those in authority over you, and in your relationships. Examples:
Refusing to admit I'm wrong. Resentment toward the Church or its teachings.
Manipulating people to get my way. Rebelling against my parents, boss, or spouse.
Insisting on controlling every detail. Looking down on others.

Fields: Pattern or behavior (textarea)


SECTION 10 — Abuse, Trauma & Wounded Responses  [Category: Wounds & Forgiveness]
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
When someone is abused or deeply traumatized, two things happen: a wound is
inflicted, and the soul learns a way to survive it. Those survival responses —
numbing, dissociation, rage, perfectionism, people-pleasing, hyper-control —
were once protective, but they often outlive the danger and keep you bound. None
of the abuse was your fault, and naming it is not weakness; it is the first step
in letting the Lord enter the place that was violated.

Note: If working through severe trauma, walk this section gently alongside a
trusted priest, counselor, or therapist — spiritual healing and professional
care work together, not against each other.

Action Item:
Without forcing yourself to relive every detail, write down the abuses and
traumas you have suffered, and beside each, the way you learned to cope with it.
Examples: Physical abuse by a parent → I keep everyone at arm's length. A
betrayal → I trust no one. A frightening accident → I try to control everything.
Sexual abuse → I shut down emotionally.

Fields: What happened (textarea), Sinful response — optional (textarea)


SECTION 11 — Addictions & Compulsions  [Category: Personal Sins]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
An addiction is rarely just about the substance or behavior — it is a
counterfeit comfort the soul reaches for to soothe a deeper ache it doesn't
know how to name. Alcohol, drugs, pornography, food, spending, screens,
gambling, work: each promises relief and delivers bondage. Compulsion is a
sign that something underneath is unhealed and unfed. Bringing these into the
light invites Christ to meet the real hunger they were trying to fill.

Action Item:
List the addictions and compulsions that have a grip on you, whether obvious or
hidden. Beside each, note what it tends to numb or soothe. Examples: Alcohol →
I drink to quiet my anxiety. Pornography → I turn to it when I feel rejected.
Compulsive eating → I eat when I'm lonely. Phone or scrolling → I escape rather
than feel. Overworking → I prove my worth.

Fields: Addiction or compulsion (textarea)


SECTION 12 — False Religion & Spiritual Mixture  [Category: Occult & Dark Powers]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Spiritual mixture is the attempt to follow Christ while also holding on to
spiritual practices, beliefs, or sources of power that do not come from Him. It
often feels open-minded or harmless — "taking the good from every path" — but
it divides loyalty and leaves a door cracked open. This includes time spent in
false religions, syncretistic devotions, and "Christian" practices borrowed from
the occult.

Action Item:
Write down any involvement with false religion or spiritual mixture, past or
present, including practices you blended into your faith. Examples: Participation
in another religion's worship or initiation. New Age or "Christianized" energy
practices. Folk-magic devotions, superstitions, or charms used alongside prayer.
Yoga or meditation tied to false spirituality. Ancestor veneration outside the
communion of saints.

Fields: Practice or religion (textarea)


SECTION 13 — Harm Done to Others  [Category: Personal Sins]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
This section turns the mirror around. We are quick to remember our wounds and
slow to remember the wounds we have caused. The harm we have done to others —
through words, betrayal, neglect, abuse of power, or simple selfishness — weighs
on the conscience and often calls for more than confession: it calls for
restitution and, where possible and prudent, reconciliation. Owning what we have
done frees both us and, sometimes, those we have hurt.

Action Item:
Make a list of people you have harmed and how. Be specific and refuse to make
excuses for yourself. Note where an apology or restitution may be owed. Examples:
My children, for years of harsh words. A former employee, for the way I treated
them. My spouse, for my infidelity. A friend I betrayed. The person I cheated
or stole from.

Fields: Harm done (textarea)


SECTION 14 — Dominant Spiritual Patterns  [Category: Personal Sins]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
When you step back and look at the whole picture — your sins, wounds, vows, and
the lies you've believed — recurring themes usually emerge. The same kind of
struggle keeps reappearing in different costumes: a spirit of fear, of rejection,
of control, of lust, of unworthiness, of heaviness. Identifying these dominant
patterns helps you stop fighting symptoms one at a time and address the root
the enemy keeps returning to.

Action Item:
Review everything you've written so far and look for the recurring threads. Name
the two or three dominant patterns that seem to run through your life. Examples:
A pattern of fear and anxiety in every season. A deep root of rejection and
never feeling wanted. Control as my answer to everything. Heaviness or despair I
can't shake. Lust as my go-to comfort.

Format: Checklist — participants select from a list of common spiritual
strongholds (fear, rejection, lust, shame, control, despair, pride, etc.)


SECTION 15 — Generational Sins & Family Patterns  [Category: Generational Lines]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Scripture speaks of sin and its consequences passing down through family lines
(Exodus 20:5), and most families carry recognizable patterns — divorce, addiction,
abuse, occult involvement, suicide, rage, poverty, infidelity — that repeat
generation after generation as if on a track. You are not guilty of your
ancestors' sins, but you can renounce their effects and refuse to pass them on.
Naming the pattern breaks the silent assumption that "this is just how our
family is."

Action Item:
Map the patterns you can see running through your family line on both sides.
Write down the recurring sins and afflictions across the generations you know of.
Examples: Alcoholism on my father's side for three generations. Repeated divorce
and abandonment. A history of occult involvement or "the gift" being passed down.
Suicide, depression, or a pattern of early death.

Fields: Pattern or sin (textarea)


SECTION 16 — Family Members & Ancestors of Concern  [Category: Generational Lines]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Sometimes a specific person in the family line stands out — an ancestor known
to have practiced witchcraft, made a pact, joined a secret society, dedicated
descendants to a false god, or lived in deep unrepentant darkness. Their choices
can cast a long shadow. This is not about judging the dead; it is about
identifying specific doorways that may need to be renounced and closed on behalf
of your line.

Action Item:
List by name (or relationship, if the name is unknown) any specific family members
or ancestors who concern you spiritually, and why. Examples: My grandmother, who
was known as the village healer/witch. An uncle who was a Freemason. An ancestor
said to have made a vow or dedication. A parent who cursed the family.

Fields: Name or description (text), Groups or sins involved — optional (textarea)


SECTION 17 — Lodges, Secret Societies & Oath-Bound Groups  [Category: Generational Lines]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Secret societies bind their members through oaths, hidden rituals, and divided
loyalties — often invoking false gods or swearing blood-curses upon themselves
and their descendants if the oath is broken. Freemasonry is the most common,
but there are many others. The Church has long warned against them precisely
because the oaths create real spiritual bondage. Renouncing membership — your
own or an ancestor's — severs those oaths and their inherited effects.

Action Item:
List any involvement, by you or your ancestors, with lodges, secret societies,
or oath-bound groups, along with the degree or rank if known. Examples:
Freemasonry (and branches such as the Shriners or Eastern Star). The Odd Fellows,
Elks, or similar lodges. College or fraternal secret societies with binding oaths.
Mafia or gang blood oaths. Any group requiring secret initiation or vows.

Fields: Group name (text), Titles/degrees/roles — optional (textarea),
        Known oaths or rituals — optional (textarea)


SECTION 18 — Agreement Sins  [Category: Personal Sins]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Some sins we don't commit ourselves — we agree with, approve of, encourage, or
fail to oppose. Scripture treats this consent seriously (Romans 1:32): standing
by while someone is harmed, cheering on another's revenge, encouraging a friend's
sin, voting for or funding grave evil, or silently approving what we knew was
wrong. Agreement joins us to the sin even when our own hands seem clean.
Renouncing these agreements withdraws your consent.

Action Item:
Write down the sins you did not commit directly but agreed with, encouraged,
enabled, or refused to stand against. Examples: Pressuring or supporting someone
through an abortion. Egging on a friend's affair or revenge. Going along with
gossip or slander to fit in. Staying silent while someone was abused or wronged.
Approving of grave evil because it benefited me.

Fields: Agreement or cooperation with evil (textarea)


SECTION 19 — Named Spirits, False Deities & Unholy Allegiances  [Category: Occult & Dark Powers]
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Idolatry and the occult are not merely abstract; they often involve specific
named spirits, false gods, or beings to whom devotion, sacrifice, or allegiance
was given — by you or your ancestors. A santo, an orisha, a "spirit guide," a
familiar spirit, a deity invoked in a ritual, a being you bargained with: each
name represents an allegiance that must be specifically renounced. The enemy
clings to ground that has never been explicitly reclaimed.

Action Item:
List any named spirits, false deities, or beings to whom you (or your family)
have given devotion, allegiance, or invitation. Examples: A "spirit guide" or
"guardian" from New Age practice. Named deities from Santería, Voodoo, Hinduism,
or folk religion. A familiar spirit invited through ritual or game. An imaginary
"friend" that took on a spiritual presence. Any being you made a deal with. Where
you don't know the name, describe the encounter.

Fields: Name of spirit or deity (text)


SECTION 20 — Objects of Concern  [Category: Occult & Dark Powers]
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Physical objects can carry spiritual significance — either because they were
dedicated to a false power, used in occult practice, or simply because they keep
a sin or allegiance alive in your home. Scripture records believers burning their
occult items as part of repentance (Acts 19:19). This is not superstition about
"haunted" objects; it is about removing what stands as a banner or invitation
for the enemy in your space.

Action Item:
Walk through your home (and storage, attic, jewelry box, bookshelves, devices)
and list objects that may need to be removed, destroyed, or blessed. Examples:
Occult or New Age books, tarot decks, Ouija boards. Idols, statues, or charms
from false religions. Items received in initiations or rituals. Pornography.
Gifts or heirlooms tied to occult or lodge membership.

Fields: Object description (textarea)


SECTION 21 — Curses, Spells & Blood Contracts  [Category: Occult & Dark Powers]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
A curse is the use of spiritual power to wish or invoke harm, and curses can be
spoken over us, inherited from our line, or even pronounced over ourselves in
moments of self-hatred. Spells, hexes, and blood contracts are deliberate acts
of the same kind. Whether you fear someone cursed you, suspect a generational
curse, or recognize the harsh words you've spoken over yourself or your children,
these can be renounced and broken in the authority of Christ.

Action Item:
List any curses, spells, or blood contracts you are aware of — spoken over you,
inherited, made by you, or made against others. Examples: A relative who said
they would curse the family. Words spoken over me as a child. A spell I cast or
had cast for me. Curses I've spoken over myself. A blood pact or written contract.

Fields: Kind (Curse against family / Blood contract or ritual bond),
        Description (textarea)


SECTION 22 — Infirmities  [Category: Generational Lines]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Not every illness is spiritual — but some physical and emotional afflictions are
tied to wounds, sins, generational patterns, or oppression, and persist despite
every natural remedy. Listing them is not a diagnosis and never a replacement
for medical care; it is bringing your body and mind before the Healer and asking
whether anything in this workbook may be connected to what you suffer.

Action Item:
Write down the physical and emotional infirmities you carry, especially those
that are chronic, unexplained, or that "run in the family." Note any that began
after a specific event. Examples: Lifelong anxiety or depression. Chronic pain
or illness no doctor can explain. A recurring affliction also suffered by my
parent or grandparent. Insomnia or torment that started after an occult experience.

Fields: Infirmity or condition (text),
        "I am specifically asking God for healing of this" (yes/no)


SECTION 23 — Future Generation Concerns  [Category: Generational Lines]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
Just as sin and wounds can pass down a family line, so can blessing. Having
worked to close the doors of your own life, this final section turns toward
those who come after you — your children, grandchildren, and descendants not
yet born. Here you renounce, on their behalf, the patterns you refuse to pass
on, and you intentionally speak blessing and protection over your line. You
become the one in whom the old pattern stops and the new inheritance begins.

Action Item:
Name the patterns, curses, or bondages you most want to ensure are not passed
to your children and descendants, and write a blessing you want to speak over
them instead. Examples: "The addiction in our family stops with me." "No more
divorce and abandonment in this line." "I renounce the occult inheritance on
behalf of my children."

Fields: Concern (textarea)


SECTION 24 — Soul Ties & Unhealthy Bonds  [Category: Wounds & Forgiveness]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
A soul tie is an ungodly spiritual bond formed when two souls become joined in
a way that gives one person an unhealthy hold over another. These bonds differ
from ordinary forgiveness work (Section 2) and sexual sin (Section 4) — they
are persistent attachments where a person still has a grip on you long after the
relationship ended or the harm was done. They can form through sexual union,
trauma, emotional dependency, spiritual abuse, grief, occult ritual, or any
relationship where control, idolatry, or fear created a bond stronger than it
should have been. The sign is the "pull" — the way a person still occupies space
in your mind, emotions, or decisions even when you know they shouldn't.

Action Item:
Ask the Holy Spirit to show you the people who still have a hold on you — people
you find yourself mentally rehearsing conversations with, emotionally tethered
to, or unable to move freely past. Write down each person, how the bond was
formed, and whether you feel that pull is still active.

Fields: Who still has a hold on you? (text),
        How did this bond form? (Sexual / Abusive or controlling / Trauma bond /
          Emotional dependence or idolatry / Family or parental control /
          Addiction companion / Grief or the dead / Occult or ritual / Other),
        "Does this person still have a grip on you, even after you've forgiven them?" (yes/no)

PRAYER 1 — PERSONAL DELIVERANCE PRAYER  (17 pages)
================================================================================

HOW THE PRAYERS WORK:
The participant's workbook entries are woven into the prayer text at the
places marked below as [PARTICIPANT'S ENTRIES]. Everything else is verbatim
prayer text read aloud.

────────────────────────────────────
Page 1 — Opening Prayer
────────────────────────────────────
Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, I come before You in humility, faith,
and repentance. I believe that You are my Savior, my Redeemer, my Lord, and my
Deliverer. I believe that You died for my sins, that You rose from the dead, and
that Your Precious Blood is stronger than every sin, wound, curse, demon, false
agreement, unholy bond, and evil influence that has touched my life.

In Your holy name, and through the power of Your Precious Blood, I renounce
Satan, all his works, and all his empty promises. I reject every evil that I
have accepted through sin, ignorance, fear, rebellion, weakness, curiosity,
woundedness, or deliberate choice. I reject every evil that has entered my life
through my own sins, through sins committed against me, through the sins of my
family line, through occult practices, through unholy relationships, through
false vows, through unforgiveness, through trauma, through deception, or through
any other opening known or unknown to me.

Lord Jesus, I ask You to cover me with Your Precious Blood. Wash over my mind,
my body, my soul, my memory, my imagination, my heart, my home, my family, my
relationships, and everything entrusted to me. Let every place of darkness be
brought under Your authority. Let every place of bondage be brought under Your
Cross. Let every place of fear, shame, confusion, hatred, lust, pride, rebellion,
despair, or torment be touched by Your mercy.

Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God, spread your mantle over me and pray for me.
Saint Michael the Archangel, defend me in battle. Holy angels of God, surround
me, guard me, and drive far from me every evil spirit. All holy saints of God,
pray for me.

Lord Jesus, as I name these sins, wounds, bonds, and agreements, give me true
repentance, true renunciation, and true freedom. Amen.

────────────────────────────────────
Page 2 — Personal Sins
────────────────────────────────────
Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of all my personal sins, especially [SECTION 1
ENTRIES]. I confess that these sins were wrong, and I do not excuse them, hide
them, defend them, or cling to them. I place them under Your Precious Blood and
ask You to forgive me, cleanse me, and give me the grace to make a good
Confession wherever Confession is needed.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce these sins. I reject every pleasure,
habit, false comfort, false identity, and false promise attached to them. I break
every agreement I have made with these sins, whether knowingly or unknowingly,
and I reject every spirit that has gained influence in my life through them.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I ask Almighty God to bind every evil spirit connected to these sins, to break
its power over me, to cast it away from me, and to remove every claim,
attachment, influence, or foothold it has had in my life. Lord Jesus, close every
door I opened through these sins, and fill every place that has been emptied
with Your Holy Spirit.

────────────────────────────────────
Page 3 — Unforgiveness, Resentment, and Judgment
────────────────────────────────────
Lord Jesus Christ, I repent of all unforgiveness, bitterness, resentment, anger,
hatred, revenge, accusation, and sinful judgment. I acknowledge before You the
people I have refused to forgive, especially [SECTION 2: NAMES OF PEOPLE TO
FORGIVE].

What happened to me mattered. The wounds were real. The injustice was real. But
I do not want unforgiveness to rule my heart, and I do not want bitterness to
give the evil one any place in me. Therefore, in obedience to You, and with the
help of Your grace, I choose to forgive those who hurt me. I release them into
Your hands. I release my demand for revenge. I release the debt they owe me, and
I ask You to judge rightly, heal deeply, and restore what can be restored.

In the holy name of Jesus, I renounce unforgiveness, bitterness, resentment,
hatred, revenge, accusation, and every sinful judgment I have made against others.
I break every unholy bond, attachment, agreement, and spiritual claim formed
through unforgiveness. I reject every spirit that has used these wounds to keep
me bound.

In the holy name of Jesus Christ, and through the power of His Precious Blood,
I ask Almighty God to bind every spirit of unforgiveness, bitterness, hatred,

PRAYER 3 — DAILY PRAYER  (14 pages)
================================================================================

Note: This is intended as an ongoing daily protection prayer that participants
continue using after the program concludes. It has live in-prayer text areas where
the participant names specific sins, forgiveness needs, and concerns on any given day.

────────────────────────────────────
Page 1 — Opening
────────────────────────────────────

Son. I come to You through the new covenant in His Precious Blood, asking for
mercy, protection, healing, and the grace to remain faithful to You.

Lord Jesus Christ, I ask You to help me deny myself, take up my cross, and follow
You. Help me to die to sin, resist temptation, be led by the Holy Spirit, and no
longer conform myself to the patterns of this world. Teach me to seek Your will
in every decision, to remain close to You in prayer, and to take every thought
captive in obedience to Christ.

Father, please provide for me and my family in every way we need. Provide for
our souls, our bodies, our relationships, our work, our health, our finances, our
home, and our daily responsibilities. Protect us from evil. Bless us with the
grace to pursue You, listen to You, obey You, and glorify You in all that we do.

Blessed Virgin Mary, cover us with your mantle. Saint Michael the Archangel,
defend us in battle. Holy angels of God, surround us, guard us, and keep far
from us every evil spirit.

────────────────────────────────────
Page 2 — Daily Repentance and Forgiveness
────────────────────────────────────

prayer. I repent especially of [TODAY'S SINS — participant enters in the moment].

I place these sins under the Precious Blood of Jesus, and I ask You to forgive
me, cleanse me, and lead me to Confession wherever Confession is needed.

I also forgive every person who has hurt me, offended me, disappointed me,
rejected me, accused me, misunderstood me, betrayed me, or sinned against me

FORGIVENESS — participant enters in the moment].

resentment, revenge, accusation, and bitterness. I place each person into Your

agreement, and spiritual claim formed through sin, anger, unforgiveness, fear,
lust, control, manipulation, dependency, or any other unhealthy connection. I

restore every relationship to Your holy order.
